# Match language to teaching purpose

Choose the sentence's language by what the passage should help the learner understand or do.

- Explain when building understanding. State what happens, why it matters, and how ideas connect.
- Connect action to reasoning when demonstrating. Make the purpose and result of each step visible.
- Direct when guiding practice. Make the learner's next action clear.
- Match the immediate passage. One learning experience can move between explanation, demonstration, and practice.

Purpose governs the language. Softening a command does not turn it into an explanation, and an explanation alone may leave a learner unsure what to do. Supply the understanding or direction the moment requires.
