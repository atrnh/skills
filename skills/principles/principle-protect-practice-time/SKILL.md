description: "Apply when designing or revising exercises, labs, code jams, projects, or other learner practice. Reduce incidental work so learners spend their time practicing the intended skill, with clear expectations for the result."
---

# Protect practice time

Spend the learner's effort on the judgment or skill the activity is meant to develop. Remove obstacles that consume time without contributing to that learning.

- Identify which decisions learners need to make for themselves.
- Supply starting points or examples when inventing them is incidental to the objective.
- Remove requirements whose learning value does not justify their cost within the available time.
- Make expected outcomes and deliverables concrete, especially when many valid solutions are possible.
- Preserve room for learner choice without making an open-ended task depend on guessing what counts as success.

Difficulty is useful when it belongs to the target skill. Do not supply the very reasoning learners need to practice, remove necessary safety checks, or simplify away conditions they must learn to handle. Reduce incidental work while preserving the intended challenge.
