# The Salt House: private notes

- Rafe left because of Dad. This never goes on the page; Nell doesn't find out in this story.
- Mum kept the letters out of loyalty to Rafe, not cruelty. Readers should come round to her by the end of the sequence.
- Aunt Maud also knew. She matters in story three.
- The oars are the tell. A first reader should only catch it in hindsight.
