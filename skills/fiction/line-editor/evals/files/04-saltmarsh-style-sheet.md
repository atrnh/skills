# Style sheet: Saltmarsh

- Narration is present tense, close third on Tessa.
- Spelling: "grey," not "gray."
- Invented terms are lowercase, not italicized, and never glossed. The reader learns them from context: "tidewife," "the drownfolk."
- Aunt Del's dialogue drops final g's, with apostrophes ("goin'," "nothin'"). Everyone else's dialogue uses standard spelling.
