# Format by meaning

Formatting tells readers how to interpret text. Choose it according to the text's role in the sentence and the document's conventions.

- Identify the role before choosing the style (a reference, a term, exact input, and emphasis serve different purposes).
- Preserve exactness where it matters (literal formatting signals that the characters themselves are significant).
- Make emphasis earn its place (visual weight should reflect meaningful importance).
- Follow the document's conventions (consistent signals help readers interpret the text).
- Judge each occurrence in context (identical text can serve different roles).

Consistency means giving the same meaning the same treatment. Different meanings may need different formatting, even when the words look alike. The principle guides interpretation; the medium and local conventions determine the syntax.
